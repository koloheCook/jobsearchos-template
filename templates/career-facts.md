# Career facts (template)

**Date:** 2026-10-21 (template; change to the date you fill in your copy)
**Model, effort:** Claude Opus 5.5, default effort (template; your copy can say "written by hand")

Copy this file to `data/career-facts.md` and fill it in from your resume. `data/` is gitignored and the commit guard blocks it, so your copy never enters git. jd-intake reads `data/career-facts.md` to score the You-fit-role axis; without it, that score is a placeholder.

Rules: copy facts literally from your resume or your own records. Every number (team size, budget, percent, count) must be one you can back up; if you are unsure of a number, leave it out. Anything written here can be cited in an assessment as sourced evidence.

## Roles

One block per role, most recent first.

### Title, Company (start YYYY-MM to end YYYY-MM or "present")

- Scope: team size, budget, region, who you reported to
- What you owned: the processes, programs or systems that were yours
- Results: outcomes, each with its number and where it is recorded

## Skills and tools

- Tool or method: where you used it, roughly how long

## Education and certifications

- Degree or certificate, school or issuer, year

## Anything else that counts as evidence

- Volunteer work, a business you ran, publications, talks
