# meeting-brief

One page the owner walks into the call with: posture, opener, questions, the
expected key question with three beats, a defined ask, traps, and a walk-out-with
checklist. Objectives are embedded so the debrief can score them.

## Hard precondition
Ask for the owner's objectives for THIS meeting (2-3, written) before anything
else. No objectives, no brief (PRD principle 5). Also confirm: contact name/role,
company, meeting number (first conversation vs follow-on), duration.

## Steps
1. Read the opportunity's folder end to end: `jd.md`, `assessment.md` (respect its
   gate status), `notes.md`, ALL prior `meetings/*/brief.md` + `debrief.md` (prior
   debriefs' coaching problems become this brief's Traps section).
2. Research inputs: whatever the owner pastes (profile, posts, news) plus
   `playbook/` if it exists. Do not fabricate company facts; anything unverified is
   labeled "unverified" or omitted (ASK-DONT-GUESS).
3. Write `meetings/<date>_<slug>/brief.md` per `templates/meeting-brief.md`:
   - Cluster/beat answers pull from playbook modules when they exist; otherwise
     from owner-supplied stories only. Red-team the ask: if the owner's stated goal
     reads as desperate or unfocused, say so and propose the disciplined version.
   - Traps: from prior debriefs' named coaching problems, not generic advice.
4. Close with: printed-or-loaded reminder, and "same-day debrief after the call"
   (the debrief skill scores these objectives).

## Bounds
- In-session only (needs the owner; not scheduler-safe).
- Never produce tailored application materials from here; that is gated by the
  assessment verdict, not the meeting cycle.
- No em dashes.
